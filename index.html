CalendarAgendaSync

A personal Windows desktop app that copies my work Outlook calendar into my own Google Calendar.

What it does

CalendarAgendaSync is part of Calendar Agenda, a small desktop panel that shows my day's Outlook calendar. When I switch the Google copy on, it keeps a one-way copy of my Outlook calendar in a separate Google calendar named "Outlook (Acclime)", so my work meetings also show up on my phone and in my personal calendar.

  • New Outlook events are added to that Google calendar, changed events are updated, and deleted, cancelled or declined events are removed.
  • It is one way only. Nothing is read from my other Google calendars, and nothing flows back to Outlook.
  • It asks Google only for permission to create its own calendar and manage the events in that calendar (the calendar.app.created scope). It cannot see or change any other calendar in the Google account.
  • It runs only on my own computer. There is no server and no website behind it.

Who it is for

This is a personal tool for my own use. It is not offered as a service to other people.
